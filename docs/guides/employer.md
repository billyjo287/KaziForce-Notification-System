# KaziForce for employers: a short guide

KaziForce helps you find workers for short jobs quickly. When you post a job, the right workers
hear about it at once, and you can see who got the alert and who saw it.

## 1. Create your account

1. Open KaziForce and tap **Get started**.
2. Choose **I'm hiring**, then type your name, email and a password (twice).
3. Add your phone number and confirm it with the 6-digit SMS code, so we can reach you when
   someone applies. You can skip this and do it later in **Settings**.
4. Add your company name and place under **Settings > Company profile**.

## 2. Post a job

1. Tap **My jobs**, then **Post a job**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Job title**, for example "Warehouse packers needed today".
   - **What will the worker do?** Hours, place and what to bring.
   - **Where is the job?** and the **main skill needed**.
   - **Pay** (optional), for example "KSh 1,200 per day".
   - **Closing date and time**: applications close then.
   - **Is this urgent?** Choose **Yes, I need someone soon** if you need someone within hours.
3. Tap **Post the job**.

We send the job to workers in the same place or with the same skill. If the job is urgent, they
get it on WhatsApp or SMS as well as in the app.

## 3. See who got your job alert

Open a job under **My jobs**. At the top, **Who got your job alert** shows how many workers we
told and how many have seen it, for example "Seen by 8 of 12 workers". For privacy you see the
numbers only, not the names.

## 4. Choose a worker

Under the job you see everyone who applied, with their place, skills and note.

- Tap **Accept** or **Reject**. The worker gets an alert. If you tapped by mistake, tap
  **Undo** in the message at the bottom of the screen.
- Under each applicant you can see whether they **saw** your latest update and your last
  message.
- Tap **Message** (with their name) to write to the applicant.

## 5. Your alerts

The **Alerts** page tells you when someone applies or writes to you. Every alert is marked
**Urgent**, **Important** or **For later**, with a colour, a picture and the word. See the
[worker guide](worker.md#2-your-alerts) for what each one means.

## 6. Settings

Settings are the same as for workers: where urgent alerts go first, quiet hours, the daily
summary email, language, text size and screen colours. See the
[worker guide](worker.md#5-settings).

## Good to know

- **Never ask workers for money** (a "registration fee" or similar). KaziForce blocks messages
  that look like this, and an admin may suspend the account.
- An admin can remove a job that breaks the rules; you will see the reason under **My jobs**.
- **Delete my account** (in Settings) deletes your account, your jobs and your messages after
  14 days. You can cancel by logging in before then.
