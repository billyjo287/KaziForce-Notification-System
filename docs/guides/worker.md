# KaziForce for workers: a short guide

KaziForce helps you find short jobs near you and makes sure you hear about urgent ones in time.
It works on any phone or computer with a web browser. You do not need to install an app.

## 1. Create your account

1. Open KaziForce and tap **Get started**.
2. Choose **I'm looking for work**, then type your name, email and a password. Type the
   password twice so we know it is right.
3. **Your phone number.** Type your Kenyan mobile number and tick the box to agree to receive
   messages. We send you a 6-digit code by SMS. Type it in and tap **Confirm**.
4. **How should we reach you?** Tell us if you use WhatsApp on this number, and where you check
   messages most.
5. **How much should we tell you?** Pick one. If you are not sure, choose **Recommended**.

You can skip steps 3 to 5 and do them later in **Settings**.

## 2. Your alerts

The **Alerts** page shows everything KaziForce tells you. Every alert has a colour, a picture
and a word, so you always know how important it is:

| Word | What it means | How it reaches you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Urgent** | Act now. It can expire soon (for example a job that starts in an hour). | In the app **and** on WhatsApp or SMS straight away |
| **Important** | Worth a look today (for example a message from an employer). | In the app and by email |
| **For later** | No rush (for example news from KaziForce). | In the app, and in one short email each morning |

- Tap an alert to read it. It then counts as read.
- If an alert does not matter to you, tap **Not important to me**. We will show you fewer like
  it.
- New alerts appear by themselves while the page is open. You do not need to refresh.

**If you miss an urgent alert:** we first send it to your first choice (for example WhatsApp).
If you do not open it within 10 minutes, we send it to your next choice (for example SMS).

**WhatsApp and SMS messages never contain names or details.** They only say what kind of alert
it is and give a link. Log in to see the details. This protects you if your phone is lost.

## 3. Find a job and apply

1. Tap **Jobs**. Choose a place or a skill to see fewer jobs.
2. Tap a job to read about it: what you will do, where, the pay and when it closes.
3. Tap **Apply for this job**. You can add a short note, for example when you can start.
4. Under **My applications** you see each job you applied for and what the employer decided.
   We also send you an alert when they decide.

## 4. Messages

When you apply, you can write to the employer under **Messages**. When they write to you, you
get an alert.

## 5. Settings

Open **Settings** to change:

- **Where urgent alerts go first**: use the arrow buttons to put WhatsApp, SMS or email first.
- **I use WhatsApp**: switch it off if you do not use WhatsApp on your number.
- **Send urgent alerts on both**: your first and second choice get urgent alerts at the same
  time.
- **How much should we tell you?**: the three choices from sign-up.
- **Customise**: switch each channel on or off, and choose what it gets. Here you also find:
  - **Quiet hours** (for example 21:00 to 07:00, Kenya time): only urgent alerts reach you then.
    Other messages wait until the morning.
  - **Daily summary email**: at 8:00 in the morning, the less important things from the day
    before. No news, no email.
- **Language** (English or Kiswahili), **Text size** (Normal or Large), **Screen colours**
  (light, dark or the same as your phone) and **Reduce motion**.

Every change saves at once. If you change something by mistake, tap **Undo**.

**To stop WhatsApp messages,** reply **STOP** (or ACHA) to any of our WhatsApp messages. Reply
**START** to get them again, or switch WhatsApp back on in Settings.

## 6. Logging out and deleting your account

- **Log out** is at the top of every page.
- **Log out of all devices** (in Settings) logs you out everywhere, for example if you lost your
  phone.
- **Delete my account** (in Settings) deletes your account and everything in it after 14 days.
  We email you to confirm. If you change your mind, log in before then and tap **Keep my
  account**. In alerts other people got from you, your name is then shown as "a former user".

## Need help?

- Forgot your password? On the log-in page tap **Forgot your password?** and we email you a
  link.
- If an alert asks you to **send money** to get a job, it is a scam. KaziForce never asks for
  money. We try to stop these messages before they reach you.
